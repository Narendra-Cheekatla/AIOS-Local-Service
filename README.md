\# AIOS - All In One Service



\### Local Services. Local People. One Platform.



AIOS is a local service marketplace that connects customers with nearby independent service providers.



Customers can discover service providers, view their services, prices, availability and ratings, and book services directly.



Service providers can register their skills, set their prices and availability, and accept service requests from customers.



\## Core Features



\- Customer Registration \& Login

\- Service Provider Registration \& Login

\- JWT Authentication

\- Customer Profile

\- Provider Profile

\- Service Management

\- Provider Availability

\- Service Booking

\- Booking Status Management

\- Ratings \& Reviews

\- Admin Management
